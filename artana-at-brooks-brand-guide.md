## SLIDE 5: Voice and Tone

Tone: **Friendly, Refined, Helpful**

- Position Artana as high end while staying approachable. The team is working to move past the assumption that it is affordable housing, so copy should point to the finishes, design, and amenity space.
- Not overly youthful. Emojis are fine in moderation.
- "Y'all" is welcome since the property is in San Antonio.
- Goal right now: brand awareness. Artana is new and building its reputation from scratch, slightly off the beaten path on the South Side.
- Photos of people should show diversity.

## Notes for the team (not for the slide)

- **Language flags on the website.** The site uses "Elevated living starts here," "luxury living," and "Work & Meet in Luxury" in image captions. These are on our prohibited list, so do not carry them into social copy.
- **Unit count.** NRP lists 349 units. One groundbreaking article says 249. This guide uses 349.
- **Colors not included.** #002B49 and #0072C6 also appear in the site code, but they did not show up as brand variables, so I left them out. Confirm with the client or the logo files if you want them as accents.
- **Logo.** The site serves SVG logos (main, sticky header, footer, mobile). Download them from the client's Dropbox or the site rather than recreating them.
- **Branded hashtags.** None were provided in the brief. Ask Zoe Adams before assigning one.
- **Credentials.** The Airtable onboarding record contains the Instagram login. I left it out of this guide on purpose.
- **Brief contacts.** Zoe Adams (Marketing) and Gina Lara, both at NRP Group.
